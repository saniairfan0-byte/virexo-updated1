Process

How we work with clients

A transparent, four-stage process that keeps you informed from the first conversation to long-term support.

01

Discovery & Planning

We start with a conversation about your goals, users, and constraints. From there we map out scope, timeline, and the right technical approach — so there are no surprises later.

  • 🗒️
    Requirements gatheringUnderstanding what success looks like for you.
  • 📐
    Scope & timelineA clear plan before any development begins.
02

Design & Development

Our designers and developers work in parallel — turning approved designs into working software with regular check-ins so you can give feedback early.

  • 🎨
    Interface designWireframes and visual design, reviewed with you.
  • 🧑‍💻
    Iterative buildsWorking software delivered in stages, not one big reveal.
03

Testing & Launch

Before anything goes live, we test across browsers and devices, fix what needs fixing, and plan a smooth, low-risk launch.

  • 🧪
    Cross-device QADesktop, tablet, and mobile checks before launch.
  • 🚀
    Launch supportWe're there when it goes live, watching for issues.
04

Ongoing Support

Launch isn't the end. We offer maintenance plans for updates, monitoring, and improvements as your product and business evolve.

  • 🛠️
    MaintenanceUpdates and fixes to keep things running smoothly.
  • 📊
    IterationNew features as your product grows.
